Who We Are

Builders shouldn't have to choose between quality and speed

Dice Concrete Solutions is a concrete services provider built around one idea: builders shouldn't have to choose between quality and speed. We specialise in rectifications, detailed finishing, and Class 1 & Class 2 patching — delivered by crews who show up ready to work and communicate every step of the way.

We work alongside builders and construction teams as a genuine extension of the crew, not just another name on the subcontractor list.

Our Approach

Mobilise fast. Work to spec. Keep the builder informed.

Every project starts with the same standard: mobilise fast, work to spec, and keep the builder informed. Whether it's a residential patch-up or a multi-level commercial rectification, we bring the same discipline and attention to detail to every job — because your reputation with your client depends on the work we do for you.
