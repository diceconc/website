Who We Are

Concrete work that keeps your schedule intact

Dice Concrete Solutions is a trusted provider of high-quality concrete services, specialising in rectifications, detailed finishing, and Class 1 & Class 2 patching to the highest industry standards. We work alongside builders and construction teams, delivering skilled labour with efficiency, precision and reliability — so your project stays on schedule without compromising quality.

Which areas do you service?

We work with builders and construction teams across the Gold Coast, Brisbane and Sydney.

What's the difference between Class 1 and Class 2 patching?

Class 1 is structural patching and Class 2 is cosmetic. Our crews are experienced across both and match the work to the tolerance your project requires — getting the class right the first time saves you from failed inspections and rework.

How quickly can you mobilise a crew?

When your schedule is on the line, we move fast. Our crews are ready to be on site when you need them — not weeks later.

Do you work on commercial and government sites?

Yes. We have experience across residential, commercial and government/defence sites, including work carried out at RAAF Base.

Will you keep our site clean?

A clean site isn't just about appearances — it's safer, faster, and better for every trade working alongside us. We leave your site the way we found it, minus the defect.
