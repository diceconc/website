01 Rectification

Concrete Rectifications & Defect Repairs

Cracking, spalling or structural defects don't fix themselves — and left alone, they only get more expensive. Our team diagnoses and rectifies concrete defects to spec, restoring structural integrity without unnecessary downtime on site.

Getting the patching class right the first time saves you from failed inspections and rework. Our crews are experienced across both Class 1 (structural) and Class 2 (cosmetic) patching, matched to the tolerance your project requires.
